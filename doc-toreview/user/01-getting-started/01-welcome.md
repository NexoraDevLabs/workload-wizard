# 🎓 Welcome to WorkloadWizard

**Your Complete Guide to Getting Started**

---

## What is WorkloadWizard?

**WorkloadWizard** is a smart, role-based platform that helps universities and colleges **plan, track, and manage staff workloads** with accuracy and ease.

It replaces complex spreadsheets with a structured, intuitive system for managing **courses, modules, groups, lecturers, and academic year planning** — all in one place.

## Who is it for?

- **Organisation Administrators** – Manage staff, courses, modules, groups, and workload allocations within their institution.
- **Professional Leads & Management** – Manage staff, courses, modules, groups and workload allocations within their teams.
- **Lecturers & Staff** – View their personal workload breakdown, allocations, and upcoming teaching schedules.

## Key Features

✅ **Role-Based Access** – Fine-grained control over what users can view and edit.
✅ **Multi-Year Planning** – Work on future academic years without disrupting the current year.
✅ **Hours Tracking** – Automatically calculate teaching and admin hours against contracted limits.
✅ **Flexible Structure** – Adaptable to departments, faculties, or entire institutions.

## How to Get Started

1️⃣ **Log In** – Use your provided credentials to access WorkloadWizard.
2️⃣ **Set Your Academic Year** – Choose the year you want to work on (e.g., 2025/26).
3️⃣ **Add Staff** – Create staff profiles with their roles and contracted hours.
4️⃣ **Create Courses & Modules** – Build your academic structure.
5️⃣ **Assign Groups & Lecturers** – Allocate teaching and admin hours based on your workload policies.

## Pro Tips for Success

💡 **Start with the essentials** – Get your staff, courses, and modules in place first.
💡 **Keep roles organised** – Use default permissions as a base, then tweak as needed.
💡 **Plan ahead** – Draft future academic years early to smooth the transition.
